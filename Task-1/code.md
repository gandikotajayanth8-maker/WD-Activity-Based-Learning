 ##  NutriLife – Healthy Food Webpage

NutriLife is a simple and user-friendly healthy food website designed to encourage people to follow healthy eating habits and maintain a balanced lifestyle. The webpage is developed using HTML and CSS.

It contains four main sections:

1. Home: Introduces the NutriLife website with the slogan "Eat Well, Live Well" and an Explore Recipes button.
2. Healthy Recipes: Displays four healthy food recipes, including Quinoa Salad, Chicken and Broccoli, Berry Overnight Oats, and Mango Chia Pudding, along with their ingredients and preparation times.
3. Weekly Meal Plan: Provides a seven-day meal plan with breakfast, lunch, and dinner suggestions.
4. Health Tips: Provides useful tips about drinking water, eating fruits and vegetables, avoiding junk food, eating balanced meals, and exercising regularly.

The webpage uses attractive colours, navigation links, recipe cards, and a table to present information clearly. CSS is used to improve the appearance and layout of the website.

Conclusion: NutriLife helps users learn about healthy recipes, plan their daily meals, and understand the importance of good nutrition. It provides a simple and attractive platform for promoting healthy food habits.
